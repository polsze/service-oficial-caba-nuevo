
# Service Oficial CABA — Website

Corporate website developed for **Service Oficial CABA**, a technical service company specializing in the repair and maintenance of household appliances and electronic equipment.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://serviceoficial-caba.com.ar/

## 📌 About the Project

The website was developed to establish a professional online presence, showcase the company's technical services, and make it easier for customers to request assistance.

The company provides technical services across Buenos Aires City (CABA), Greater Buenos Aires (GBA), and other areas of Argentina.

## 🚀 Main Features

- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- Service categories for household appliances and electronic equipment.
- Brand and product information.
- Geographic service coverage sections.
- Direct WhatsApp contact integration.
- Customer inquiry and contact information.
- SEO-oriented content and structure.
- Clear navigation and user-focused interface.

## 🔧 Services

- Washing machines and dryers.
- Refrigerators and freezers.
- Air conditioning systems.
- Electric ovens and microwaves.
- Televisions and monitors.
- Dishwashers and water heaters.
- Small household appliances.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Strengthen the company's digital presence.
- Present technical services in an organized way.
- Improve customer access to service information.
